# Script

Every line a character says, scene by scene, in simple words, English and Danish. It follows `story.md`: nothing
happens here that the story doesn't say. **Draft:** Brian's to change. Anything marked *[open]* waits on the story.

Lines are short enough to read in a speech bubble at a glance: a few words, no more.
