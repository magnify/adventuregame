# Puzzle tree

What she finds, where, and what it opens. Her pocket holds up to three things (the key and the flower can be there together). Each step follows the one before it,
so a child can never get stuck with the wrong thing. **Draft:** anything not in `story.md` is marked *(new)*
and needs Brian's yes.

```
STREET
  mat ──lift──▶ key ──use on──▶ door in the tree ──▶ MEADOW
  window box ──pick──▶ flower (new)  ─────────────┐
                                                  │
MEADOW                                            ▼
  beetle blocks the path, shows a flower (new) ◀── give flower
        └──▶ beetle gives a firefly in a jar (new) ─┐
                                                    │
FOREST                                              ▼
  the fork: which way? ──hold up the firefly (new)──▶ it lights the narrow path
  dark ──▶ the wisps take over, the firefly goes free ──▶ SWAMP

SWAMP
  stuck in the mud ──tap the small stick, then Barlin──▶ he makes it big ──▶ she's out
        └──▶ stick in her pocket (her first weapon) ──▶ kept for later [open]

WITCH'S HOUSE [open]
  "Don't eat, drink is fine": take the cup, push the bun away
```

Order in the street: she can pick the flower any time before the door. If she goes through without it, the flower
is growing in the meadow too, so nothing is ever missed.
