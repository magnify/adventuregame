# Style trial: the street

The first step of the restart. One place, four looks, pick one. Everything after this is drawn in
the look that wins.

**Picked: C, paper cut-out** (Lumino City as the reference). Easiest to keep consistent across
generated pictures, and it matches the paper puppets the game already animates. Next: street
pictures in this look, then reference sheets for the girl and Barlin.

## How to run it

Put each prompt below into your image generator, two or three times each, and send back the best
one or two of each look. Same scene every time, so the only thing that changes is the style.

- Ask for the widest shape the tool offers (21:9 or 2:1). The street is a wide scene. If the tool
  only does 16:9, that's fine for judging a style.
- If a picture has people in it, or writing on it, throw it away and try again. The girl goes in
  later, drawn separately.
- Don't fix details. We're judging the look, not the tree.
- Tell us which tool you used. If it can take a reference image, that's how we'll keep every later
  picture in the same style.

## The scene (the same in every prompt)

> a quiet residential street in a small European town, early morning, a row of narrow townhouses
> in soft colours along the back, a paved pavement in front, a street lamp, a cat sitting on the
> pavement. On the right, a big old leafy tree with a thick trunk and one strong side branch
> reaching out to the left at about half height. In the trunk, just above the branch, a tiny
> arched wooden door with a keyhole, and a little red striped doormat in front of it. Side view,
> flat stage-like composition like a theatre set, the ground a straight line across the lower
> fifth of the picture. No people, no text.

## The four looks

**A. Painted storybook.** Bold simple shapes, warm saturated colour, visible brush texture.
Closest in feel to Lost in Play.

> [the scene], painted in gouache, bold simplified shapes, warm saturated colours, soft visible
> brush texture, clean silhouettes, whimsical children's adventure game background, gentle
> morning light

**B. Ink and watercolour.** Fine wobbly ink lines and loose washes, lots of paper showing.
Closest in feel to Pilgrims.

> [the scene], fine hand-drawn ink line with a slightly wobbly pen, loose watercolour washes,
> lots of white paper showing through, muted earthy colours, cosy and a little odd, hand-made
> children's book illustration

**C. Paper cut-out.** Real cut and layered paper, photographed. Honest to how the characters
already move, as paper puppets.

> [the scene], made of layered cut paper and card, photographed, visible paper grain and torn
> edges, soft shadows between the layers, handmade diorama, soft pastel colours

**D. Nordic picture book.** Muted, quiet, finely detailed pencil and watercolour, soft light.
Calmer than the other three.

> [the scene], coloured pencil and soft watercolour, muted Scandinavian palette of dusty blue,
> ochre and moss green, fine detail, calm and quiet, soft diffuse light, classic Nordic picture
> book illustration

## What happens next

You pick the one you'd want her to live in. Then:

1. The same look for the girl and Barlin, as one reference sheet each, which we cut into the parts
   the game moves.
2. The street built in that look, with enough in it that most things answer a tap.
3. Judged on a real tablet next to Lost in Play. If it holds, the other three places follow.

## The anchor

`art/reference/street-anchor.webp` is the first picture in the chosen look, and the style
reference for everything after it. Right palette, right paper grain. Two things it gets wrong for
a game: it's one flat picture, where the game needs separate layers, and the tree runs off the
edge, where the girl has to be able to climb a whole tree.

## The street, piece by piece

Upload the anchor picture with each prompt and ask for a transparent background (ChatGPT can do
this). If the tool can't, ask for a plain flat white background and we'll cut it out.

Each prompt ends with: *"Same style, palette and paper texture as the reference image. Made of
layered cut paper and card, photographed, soft shadows between layers. Side view. Transparent
background. No text."*

1. **Houses:** the row of pastel townhouses only, with roofs and chimneys, window boxes and potted
   plants, no pavement, no tree, no lamp, no sky. Wide, and the left and right edges must line up
   so it can repeat.
2. **Pavement:** a strip of paving slabs, a kerb, then cobbled road, seen straight from the side.
   Wide, edges line up so it can repeat.
3. **Tree:** the whole tree, roots to crown, nothing cut off, a thick trunk and one strong branch
   reaching out to the left at half height, wide enough for a child to stand on. In the trunk just
   above the branch, an empty dark arched hole where a small door fits.
4. **Door closed:** a small arched wooden door with a keyhole and iron hinges, face-on.
5. **Door open:** the same door swung open, warm bright light pouring out of the doorway.
6. **Doormat:** a little red and white striped doormat, side view, very thin.
7. **Key:** a small old brass key, side view.
8. **Street lamp:** an old black iron street lamp, unlit, whole.
9. **Cat:** a grey and white cat sitting, side view facing left.
10. **Sky:** a soft pale morning sky with a low sun and faint rooftops and a church spire far
    away. Wide, no foreground.
