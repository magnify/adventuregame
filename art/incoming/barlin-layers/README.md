# Barlin reference layers

Cut from `art/reference/barlin-sheet.png` on the reference image's unchanged 1254 × 1254 canvas. Stack `wing-back.png`, `body.png`, `wing-front.png`, then `hat.png` in that order.

The visible paper pixels are copied from the sheet. The far wing continues beneath the near wing to the body, with extra paper at the wing roots and beneath the hat brim for small rotations. `joints.json` gives matching body sockets and part pins in reference pixel coordinates. `stacked.png` is the actual layer composite for review.
